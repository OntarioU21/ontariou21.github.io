One-click import from league websites
  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Send to Rankings (or right-click it and choose "Bookmark link")
  2. Open a league's Schedule & Results page, for example beechey.ca/Schedule.
  3. Click the bookmark. It reads every month of results on that site and opens them here for review. Nothing is saved until you press Import.

Copy a results or schedule table from a league website (select the rows, Ctrl+C) and paste it here. CSV also works: date,home,home_score,away,away_score,shootout,type. Games without a final score are skipped, and games that are already entered are flagged so they don't get added twice.

Team missing? Add it on the Teams tab, or use Import, which creates teams for you.

Backup & restore

Download a backup regularly. Restoring replaces all teams, games and settings with the file's contents (the current data is kept as a copy on the server first). Use it to move your rankings to the live site.
